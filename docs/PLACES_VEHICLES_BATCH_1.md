## Asset feasibility — stated before modelling
The collections contain PNG design sheets, not editable 3D models or dimensioned plans. Visible forms, main colours and major fixture arrangements can be authored with the current Three.js mesh tools. Hidden backs, exact dimensions and unseen vehicle cabin construction require restrained interpretation. Product labels, tiny stock, mechanical details, material texture and cinematic lighting are simplified. No reference-quality or pixel-exact claim. Real phone performance and live visual acceptance remain unverified.

The existing 10 × 9 room format and street footprints are retained for save compatibility. These constrain literal layout/scale reproduction. No invented replacement palettes: green/cream provisions and yellow/charcoal terminal retain their supplied material contrast. No new full-image texture planes masquerade as enterable rooms.

Airports, ferries, aircraft, luxury/private vehicles, government buildings, new homes and all other supplied designs remain planned, with their full roadmap intact. No new vehicle ownership, business ownership, public-property editing rights or election privileges are granted.
