FEMALE WARDROBE COLLECTION

38 original PNG design sheets organised into 12 categories.
Includes 36 hairstyle designs across six hairstyle sheets.
The rejected first fitted-jogger sheet is excluded.

These are the original generated image sheets, with multiple designs per PNG.
They have not been split into individual items or modified.
PNG alpha transparency is preserved.

These are visual design references, not ready-to-wear 3D models, UV texture
maps or rigged game assets. A game folder can store them as references;
modelling, body-size fitting and rigging are still needed for 3D gameplay.

Faith, cultural and wedding collections are starter design options, not
exhaustive coverage or universal dress requirements. Ceremonial practitioner
regalia has not been included.
