# Physical phone review — required acceptance evidence

Start with an exported backup. Browser review uses ?review=daily, a separate story. APK and browser storage are separate. Do not import over your main story without a backup.

## Compact interface

1. In landscape with browser bars visible and hidden, check the corner HUD shows place/time/money; Phone and menu open with one thumb. The central face/chest should remain clear. Menu → Needs preserves needs, home and camera tools.
2. Tap Objective to expand/collapse, then Tasks. Use a home activity: name, progress and Cancel must fit below the character. Cancel should not accidentally move the camera. Check controls around the iPhone notch/home indicator and Android cutouts.
3. Diagnostics must be absent on normal start. Phone → Settings → Show diagnostics exposes it; Hide diagnostics removes it. Portrait must show only the small rotate notice, pause movement and retain usable menus.

## Cook → plate → eat → hunger improves

1. Go to the home prep counter. Compare the jollof/beans thumbnails to the selected dish. Unpack a grocery portion; select a recipe. Inspect ingredient names/counts and collect from bag or store them in the cupboard/fridge and collect there.
2. Do three short preparations. Watch the character's hands/utensil and ingredients. Move to the cooker, stir twice at least two seconds apart, keep heat 55–75 and simmer at least eight seconds. Finish, return to prep and plate.
3. The finished meal must remain visible in hand with Eat/Serve/Store/Carry controls. Serve means place at your own dining table; it is not a social guest-serving system. Walk to the table and serve/eat. Watch the spoon approach the mouth and food portions shrink. Hunger should improve per completed bite, not when opening a package.
4. Cancel midway through a bite: no portion or hunger should change. Eat two bites, store leftovers in the fridge, reload, retrieve and finish. Confirm quantity, hunger and reward count did not replay. Check a second recipe and ensure thumbnails/food colours match.

## Order → receive → carry indoors → open → eat

1. Phone → Food: order a plate to Home. Confirm game-currency charge once. Leave through the physical home door and meet the courier.
2. Action → Talk: hear the NPC's greeting with matching subtitles. Ask which meal; hear your selected response, then the correct NPC answer. Ask for an extra plate and hear a refusal. Say “Not just yet”: order should remain waiting, with no refund/receipt.
3. Talk again and accept. Hear the player reply and NPC handover line. Watch both hands reach for the bag. The bag must move into the player's hand and remain visible after reload and entering home. No second meal on repeated input.
4. Put the package on the table, open it and take visible bites. Reload halfway through. Cancel/lock/switch apps during a handover and retry; no vanished or duplicated meal.
5. Thank the courier. Confirm the conversation ends and the courier leaves after the exchange. Set voice volume low, mute, subtitles off/on and text-only. Missing/blocked audio must still show text and allow completion.

## One street conversation and living world

1. Find Bisi on the west pavement near Home (start around x=-7,z=13). Approach, then Talk. Try greeting, work enquiry, Dispatch directions, casual chat and asking for a tour. Answers and route outcome should differ; she politely declines the tour. Try Ayo by Dispatch: he is busy and declines extra chat.
2. Watch blinking, subtle breathing, head direction, speech gestures and mouth motion. These are procedural approximations, not captured lip sync. Record any face distortion, skin gaps, hand clipping, stiff timing or accent/pronunciation problems. Characters should not all blink/idle together.
3. In Mama T's shift, watch the player acknowledge customers, handle utensils and hand over food. Nearby passers-by should walk routes and pause for their routines. Compare Battery saver/Phone/Balanced; distant actors should stop detailed animation.
4. Use activities/sleep to reach morning, afternoon, evening and night; check gradual light transitions and lamps at night. Settings → Weather/Rain effects enables a bounded rain comparison. Rain must stay outside; sound/animation must pause when backgrounded. Reload and confirm time/weather remain consistent.

## Record the result

For each journey record PASS/FAIL, device model, OS/browser/WebView version, graphics/resolution, portrait/landscape, audio output and a short video of the hands/face/food. Include the exact failing step. Use the 15-second walk measurement and note heat/slowdown after 15 minutes. Automated checks and software renders do not approve the physical experience. No actual iPhone/Android acceptance for these new changes has been claimed.
